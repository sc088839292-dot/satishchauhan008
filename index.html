Foundation · Module 01

Basic Introduction to Thermodynamics

Pehle basic concepts strong karo. Ye aage ke chapters ki foundation hain.

Topic 01

1. What is Thermodynamics?

Definition: Thermodynamics is the branch of science that deals with heat, work, energy and their transformations.

Hinglish: Is subject mein hum padhte hain ki heat aur work ke through energy kaise transfer ya transform hoti hai.

Heat ↔ Work ↔ Energy

Examples: Car engine, refrigerator, steam turbine, air conditioner aur power plant.

Topic 02

2. System, Surroundings & Boundary

  • System: The part selected for study.
  • Surroundings: Everything outside the system.
  • Boundary: Real or imaginary surface separating system and surroundings.
  • Universe: System + surroundings.

Closed system: Mass cannot cross boundary, but energy can. Example: sealed piston-cylinder.

Open system: Mass and energy can cross. Example: turbine.

Isolated system: Neither mass nor energy crosses. Example: ideal perfectly insulated sealed container.

Yaad rakho: Closed system mein mass fixed hota hai; open system mein mass enter ya exit kar sakta hai.
Topic 03

3. Properties and State

Property: A measurable characteristic, such as pressure, temperature, volume or energy.

Intensive: Amount of matter par depend nahi karti — temperature, pressure, density.

Extensive: Amount of matter par depend karti — mass, total volume, total internal energy.

Specific property: Extensive property divided by mass.

Specific volume: v = V/m
Specific internal energy: u = U/m

State: Condition of a system described by its properties.

Equilibrium: A condition with no unbalanced driving force causing spontaneous change.

Topic 04

4. Processes, Paths and Cycles

Process: Change of a system from one state to another.

Path: Sequence of intermediate states during a process.

Cycle: Processes after which the system returns to its initial state.

Quasi-static: A sufficiently slow process in which the system remains nearly in equilibrium.

Cycle: Final state = Initial state

Complete cycle mein state property ka net change zero hota hai; net heat aur work zero hona zaroori nahi.

Example: Heat engine heat leta hai, work produce karta hai aur initial operating state mein return karta hai.
Topic 05

5. Force and Energy

Force: Push or pull that can change motion.

F = m × a

F = force (N), m = mass (kg), a = acceleration (m/s²).

Weight = m × g

Kinetic energy: Motion ki wajah se energy.

KE = ½mV²

Potential energy: Height/position ki wajah se energy.

PE = mgz
Total energy: E = U + KE + PE
Topic 06

6. Pressure and Specific Volume

Pressure: Normal force per unit area.

P = F/A

SI unit: Pascal (Pa) = N/m²; 1 kPa = 1000 Pa.

Pabsolute = Patmospheric + Pgauge

Density: Mass per unit volume.

ρ = m/V

Specific volume: Volume per unit mass.

v = V/m = 1/ρ

Density ki unit kg/m³; specific volume ki m³/kg.

Absolute pressure ka reference perfect vacuum hai; gauge pressure atmospheric pressure ke relative hota hai.
Topic 07

7. Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics

Statement: If two systems are each in thermal equilibrium with a third system, they are in thermal equilibrium with each other.

Hinglish: Agar A aur B dono ka temperature C ke saath thermal equilibrium mein hai, to A aur B bhi ek-doosre ke saath thermal equilibrium mein honge.

If Tₐ = T꜀ and Tᵦ = T꜀, then Tₐ = Tᵦ

Importance: Temperature measurement aur thermometers ka basic principle.

Energy Balance · Module 03

First Law of Thermodynamics

Energy conservation, closed systems, ideal gases aur control-volume analysis.

Topic 08

1. First Law for a Closed System

Statement: Energy can neither be created nor destroyed; it can only be transferred or converted.

Heat into system positive; work done by system positive:

Q − W = ΔE
Q − W = ΔU + ΔKE + ΔPE

When KE and PE changes are negligible:

Q − W = ΔU = U₂ − U₁

Q = heat supplied, W = work done by system, ΔU = internal-energy change.

Sign convention: system ko heat milti hai to Q positive; system work karta hai to W positive.
Topic 09

2. Internal Energy and Enthalpy

Internal energy (U): Microscopic energy stored within a substance.

u = U/m

Enthalpy (H): Property defined as internal energy plus pressure-volume product.

H = U + PV
h = u + Pv

Specific internal energy u aur specific enthalpy h ki common unit kJ/kg hai.

U aur H state properties hain. Heat aur work energy transfer ke modes hain, state properties nahi.
Topic 10

3. Ideal Gas: Specific Heat

Ideal gas ke liye constant specific heats ki approximation:

Δu = Cv(T₂ − T₁)
Δh = Cp(T₂ − T₁)

Cv: Specific heat at constant volume.

Cp: Specific heat at constant pressure.

Cp − Cv = R
γ = Cp/Cv

Air ke approximate room-temperature values:

  • Cv = 0.718 kJ/(kg·K)
  • Cp = 1.005 kJ/(kg·K)
  • R = 0.287 kJ/(kg·K)
  • γ ≈ 1.4
Temperature difference ΔK aur Δ°C numerically equal hote hain. Gas laws mein absolute temperature Kelvin mein lo.
Topic 11

4. First Law as a Rate Equation

Heat, work aur energy change ko time ke respect mein express karna ho to rate form use hota hai.

Q̇ − Ẇ = dE/dt
Q̇ − Ẇ = dU/dt + d(KE)/dt + d(PE)/dt

Q̇ = heat-transfer rate; Ẇ = work-transfer rate.

1 kW = 1 kJ/s
Total energy ki unit kJ; energy-transfer rate ki unit kW hoti hai.
Topic 12

5. Conservation of Mass in Control Volume

Control volume ek selected region hai jiske boundary se mass enter ya exit kar sakta hai.

Accumulation = Inflow − Outflow
dmCV/dt = Σṁin − Σṁout

Steady state par mass accumulate nahi hota:

Σṁin = Σṁout

Single inlet/outlet ke liye ṁ₁ = ṁ₂.

ṁ = ρAV = AV/v

ṁ = kg/s, ρ = kg/m³, A = m², V = average velocity (m/s).

Topic 13

6. First Law for a Control Volume

General control-volume energy balance:

dECV/dt = Q̇ − Ẇ + Σṁin(h + V²/2 + gz) − Σṁout(h + V²/2 + gz)

h = specific enthalpy, V²/2 = specific kinetic energy, gz = specific potential energy.

Steady-flow, single-inlet/single-outlet process:

q − w = (h₂ − h₁) + (V₂² − V₁²)/2 + g(z₂ − z₁)

q aur w per unit mass hain. Sab terms consistent units mein rakho.

Velocity aur height changes negligible hon, to q − w ≈ h₂ − h₁.
Topic 14

7. SSSF Process and Examples

SSSF: Steady-State Steady-Flow. Control-volume properties time ke saath change nahi karti aur flow rates steady rehte hain.

Examples:

  • Turbine: Fluid energy se shaft work milta hai.
  • Compressor: Fluid pressure badhane ke liye work input.
  • Nozzle: Fluid velocity badh sakti hai.
  • Diffuser: Flow velocity kam hoti hai; pressure aam taur par badhta hai.
  • Heat exchanger: Fluids ke beech heat transfer.
  • Boiler/condenser: Fluid ko heat dena ya remove karna.
q − w = Δh + Δ(V²/2) + gΔz
Numerical mein identify karo: heat transfer hai? Work hai? Velocity ya height change important hai?
Topic 15

8. USUF Process

USUF: Unsteady-State Unsteady-Flow. Control volume ke andar stored mass aur energy time ke saath change ho sakte hain.

Examples: Tank filling, tank emptying, cylinder charging.

m₂ − m₁ = min − mout
E₂ − E₁ = Q − W + Ein − Eout

Yeh integrated energy balance hai; E₂ − E₁ control volume ki stored total energy ka change hai.

Tank-filling numerical mein entering fluid ki energy ke liye aksar enthalpy h use hoti hai. Given assumptions — jaise insulated/rigid tank — clearly apply karo.
Step-by-step practice notes

Solved Numericals

Formula identify karo, values substitute karo, units ke saath answer likho.

Numerical 01

Pressure Calculation

Question: A force of 200 N acts on an area of 0.02 m². Find pressure.

Step 1: P = F/A
Step 2: P = 200/0.02
Answer: P = 10,000 Pa = 10 kPa
Numerical 02

Specific Volume and Density

Question: A mass of 5 kg occupies 0.8 m³. Find specific volume and density.

Step 1: v = V/m = 0.8/5
Specific volume = 0.16 m³/kg
Step 2: ρ = m/V = 5/0.8
Density = 6.25 kg/m³
Check: v = 1/ρ = 1/6.25 = 0.16 m³/kg.
Numerical 03

First Law: Closed System

Question: A closed system receives 500 kJ heat and does 180 kJ work. Find change in internal energy. Ignore KE and PE.

Step 1: Q − W = ΔU
Step 2: ΔU = 500 − 180
Answer: ΔU = +320 kJ

Positive answer means internal energy 320 kJ increase hui.

Numerical 04

Ideal Gas: Change in u and h

Question: Air is heated from 300 K to 500 K. Cv = 0.718 and Cp = 1.005 kJ/(kg·K). Find Δu and Δh per kg.

Step 1: ΔT = 500 − 300 = 200 K
Step 2: Δu = 0.718 × 200
Δu = 143.6 kJ/kg
Step 3: Δh = 1.005 × 200
Δh = 201 kJ/kg
Numerical 05

Steady-Flow Turbine

Question: Steam enters with h₁ = 3200 kJ/kg and exits with h₂ = 2600 kJ/kg. Assume adiabatic operation and negligible velocity/elevation changes. Find work output per kg.

Step 1: q = 0; KE and PE changes ≈ 0
Step 2: q − w = h₂ − h₁
Step 3: 0 − w = 2600 − 3200
Answer: w = 600 kJ/kg output
Numerical 06

Mass Flow Rate

Question: Water density is 1000 kg/m³, pipe area 0.01 m² and average velocity 2 m/s. Find mass flow rate.

Step 1: ṁ = ρAV
Step 2: ṁ = 1000 × 0.01 × 2
Answer: ṁ = 20 kg/s
